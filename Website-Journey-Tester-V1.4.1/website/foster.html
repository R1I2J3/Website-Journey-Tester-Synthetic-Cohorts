Temporary care

Open your paddock to a dinosaur in transition.

Foster carers provide stable, short-term spaces for dinosaurs who need rest, routine, and gentle social practice.

Register Interest

What foster carers provide

  • A secure outdoor area or approved indoor rest bay.
  • Daily enrichment such as puzzle feeders and fern bundles.
  • Calm updates for the sanctuary care team.

What we provide

  • Training, equipment, and dinosaur-specific care notes.
  • Food plans, bedding guidance, and volunteer backup.
  • Emergency advice for hiccups, honks, and overexcited stomps.
