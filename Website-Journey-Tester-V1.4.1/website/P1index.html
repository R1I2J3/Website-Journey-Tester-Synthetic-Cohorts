Safe walks, trained teams, happy dinosaurs.

Dinosaur Pangaea pairs trained volunteers with carefully matched dinosaurs for supervised walks through padded paths, fern gardens, and quiet morning circuits. Every outing is designed around temperament, size, weather, and the walker's confidence level.

Volunteer snapshot

Most walks are short, social, and highly structured.

  • Beginner walkers start with small herbivores and a mentor.
  • Morning and twilight shifts avoid hot-path foot fatigue.
  • Every dinosaur has a handling plan and comfort signal guide.
