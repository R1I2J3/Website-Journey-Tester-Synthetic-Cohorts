About the collective

We help fictional dinosaurs practise ordinary community life.

Dinosaur Pangaea is a made-up sanctuary program built for testing website UX, accessibility, and content recommendations.

Our approach

Every dinosaur is matched with a walking plan based on temperament, height, tail sweep radius, snack motivation, and confidence around scooters, umbrellas, and school crossings.

Our volunteers

Walkers, foster carers, transport helpers, enrichment makers, and adoption guides all work from clear checklists so the fictional sanctuary stays calm and consistent.

Why this page exists

This website gives your Digital Twin Marketing UX project a realistic, low-risk test surface: navigation, calls to action, forms, cards, accessibility needs, and persona-specific content are all present without copying a real organisation.
