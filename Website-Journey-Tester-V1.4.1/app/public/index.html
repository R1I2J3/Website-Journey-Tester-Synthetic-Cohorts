Important Limitation

Percentages shown in cohort mode are calculated from synthetic assessments. They are not survey results, clinical evidence, or observed behaviour from real people. V1.4.1 analyses and can safely edit copied local static HTML source; it does not execute JavaScript-heavy applications, perform real browser clicks, or visually inspect CSS/layout. Use results to identify possible UX friction and hypotheses that should later be validated with real users.
